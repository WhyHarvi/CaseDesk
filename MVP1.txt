



For the **first usable production version**, the system must support one complete lead lifecycle:

**Lead enters CRM → assigned → contacted → followed up → converted or lost → reported.**

## Minimum features required

### 1. Authentication and agency security

- Admin and employee login
- User roles: Admin, Manager, Staff
- Every record linked to `agency_id`
- Employees only see their agency’s data
- Backend determines `agency_id`; never accept it from the frontend

### 2. Universal lead intake

At minimum, support:

- Manual lead entry
- Public website lead form
- CSV import for existing leads
- API/webhook endpoint for future integrations
- “Quick Add Lead” form for phone calls, walk-ins and WhatsApp enquiries

Direct integrations with Facebook, Instagram, Ooma and WhatsApp can follow later. Initially, their leads can enter through webhooks, Zapier/Make or manual capture.

### 3. Lead information

Each lead should contain:

- Name
- Phone
- Email
- Source
- Interested service or case type
- Assigned employee
- Lead status
- Priority
- Created date
- Last contacted date
- Next follow-up date
- Notes
- Conversion or loss reason

### 4. Duplicate prevention

Before creating a lead, check:

- Phone number
- Email address
- Existing client records

Instead of silently creating duplicates, show the existing lead and allow staff to update it.

### 5. Lead pipeline

Use a simple initial pipeline:

1. New
2. Assigned
3. Contact Attempted
4. Contacted
5. Consultation Scheduled
6. Qualified
7. Not Interested
8. Follow-up Required
9. Converted
10. Lost

Do not create dozens of stages initially.

### 6. Lead assignment

- Assign leads manually
- Optional round-robin assignment
- Show who currently owns the lead
- Admin can reassign leads
- Record every assignment change

### 7. Contact and activity timeline

Every action must be recorded:

- Call attempted
- Call connected
- WhatsApp message sent
- Email sent
- Note added
- Follow-up scheduled
- Status changed
- Employee changed
- Lead converted or lost

This timeline is essential because it shows exactly what employees have done.

### 8. Follow-up system

Every active lead should have a clear next action:

- Next follow-up date and time
- Assigned employee
- Follow-up type
- Priority
- Reminder
- Pending, completed or overdue status
- Follow-up outcome

A lead should not remain active without either a next follow-up or a final outcome.

### 9. Employee work dashboard

Each employee needs a dashboard showing:

- New leads assigned today
- Follow-ups due today
- Overdue follow-ups
- Leads not contacted
- Recent activity
- Consultations scheduled
- Leads waiting for an update

The main principle should be:

> Every employee knows exactly whom to contact next.

### 10. Lead details page

One screen should show:

- Contact information
- Lead source
- Current stage
- Assigned employee
- Full activity timeline
- Notes
- Follow-ups
- Consultation information
- Conversion status
- Lost reason

### 11. Conversion to client

When a lead converts:

- Create a client record
- Link the original lead to the client
- Preserve the lead timeline
- Record conversion date
- Record the employee responsible
- Start the client intake or case workflow

Avoid manually entering the same person again.

### 12. Lost-lead tracking

When marking a lead as lost, require a reason:

- No response
- Not interested
- Price concern
- Chose another agency
- Not eligible
- Invalid contact details
- Duplicate
- Follow-up missed
- Other

This is necessary for understanding why leads are being lost.

### 13. Initial reports

The first version should show:

- Leads received
- Leads by source
- Leads by employee
- Contacted versus not contacted
- Follow-ups completed
- Overdue follow-ups
- Consultations booked
- Converted leads
- Lost leads
- Conversion rate
- Average response time
- Loss reasons

Filters should include employee, source, date range, status and service type.

### 14. Admin controls

Admin should be able to:

- Add and disable employees
- Reassign leads
- View all employee activity
- Manage lead sources
- Manage pipeline stages
- Export leads to CSV
- View audit logs

## Minimum lead sources for launch

For the initial release, capture leads from:

| Source | Initial solution |
|---|---|
| Phone calls | Quick Add Lead form |
| Walk-ins | Quick Add Lead form |
| WhatsApp | Manual entry or Zapier/Make webhook |
| Website | Direct CRM form submission |
| Facebook/Instagram | Meta lead webhook or Zapier/Make |
| Existing Excel sheets | CSV import |
| Referrals | Manual entry with referral source |

## Essential automated rules

The initial system should automatically:

- Warn about duplicate leads
- Record every status change
- Mark missed follow-ups as overdue
- Notify the assigned employee
- Flag leads that have not been contacted
- Require a loss reason
- Record conversion details
- Prevent deletion of important activity history

## Features that can wait

Do not block the first launch for:

- AI lead scoring
- Advanced marketing automation
- WhatsApp two-way messaging
- Call recording and transcription
- Complex Ooma integration
- Email campaigns
- Client portal
- Payment processing
- Advanced analytics
- Mobile application
- Multiple custom pipelines

## Initial release completion test

The first version is ready when you can successfully test this scenario:

1. A lead arrives from the website.
2. The system detects whether it already exists.
3. The lead is assigned to an employee.
4. The employee calls and records the result.
5. A follow-up is scheduled.
6. The dashboard shows the follow-up on the correct day.
7. A manager can inspect the complete timeline.
8. The lead is converted into a client or marked lost.
9. The conversion or loss appears correctly in reports.

That is the minimum system needed to stop leads from being forgotten and begin measuring employee performance and actual conversion results.
