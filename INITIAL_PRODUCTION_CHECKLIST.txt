CASEDESK IMMIGRATION CRM - INITIAL USABLE PROTOTYPE CHECKLIST

GOAL

The system should allow an immigration agency to manage a client and their case from initial intake to case completion without depending on spreadsheets or manual database changes.

MUST IMPLEMENT BEFORE LIVE USE

1. User access and agency separation
- Admin and employees can log in securely.
- Admin can add, disable, and manage employees.
- Every user and record belongs to an agency.
- Employees can only see information from their own agency.
- The backend gets agency_id from the logged-in user, never from the frontend.
- Client portal users can only see their own information.

2. Client management
- Create, search, view, and update a client.
- Store name, phone, email, address, language, and identification details.
- Check phone and email before creating a duplicate client.
- Assign a client to an employee.
- Show all cases belonging to the client.
- Keep important client records instead of permanently deleting them.

3. Case management
- Create a case for an existing client.
- Store case type, status, stage, priority, assigned employee, and important dates.
- Allow Admin or Manager to reassign a case.
- Show a clear next action for every active case.
- Allow a case to be marked completed, closed, cancelled, or inactive.
- Record important case changes in the activity history.

4. Applicants and family members
- Add the primary applicant and additional applicants or dependants.
- Store passport, date of birth, citizenship, marital status, and contact details.
- Identify the relationship of each person to the primary applicant.
- Allow applicant information to be updated from one place.
- Prevent the same applicant from being added repeatedly to the same case.

5. Immigration profile and assessment
- Record immigration history, education, work history, language results, and current status.
- Record the service or immigration program being assessed.
- Save assessment notes, eligibility result, risks, and recommendations.
- Record who completed the assessment and when.
- Keep previous assessment information available for reference.

6. Case workflow and tasks
- Provide a simple workflow for each case type.
- Show completed, current, and upcoming steps.
- Create tasks with an assigned employee, due date, priority, and status.
- Allow employees to complete, reschedule, or cancel a task.
- Show overdue tasks clearly.
- Do not leave an active case without a next action or pending task.

7. Follow-ups, reminders, and appointments
- Schedule client follow-ups with a date, time, employee, and reason.
- Mark follow-ups completed and record the outcome.
- Show follow-ups due today and overdue.
- Book, reschedule, complete, or cancel appointments.
- Show upcoming appointments on the employee dashboard.
- Record missed appointments and the next action.

8. Notes and activity timeline
- Add internal notes to clients and cases.
- Record calls, emails, messages, appointments, document changes, and status changes.
- Show who performed each action and when.
- Display all important activity in one chronological timeline.
- Do not allow normal users to edit or delete important history silently.

9. Document management
- Upload documents to the correct client and case.
- Use clear document names and document types.
- Show document status: Requested, Uploaded, Under Review, Changes Requested, Approved, or Finalized.
- Record who uploaded and reviewed each document.
- Allow reviewers to add comments or request a replacement.
- Prevent one agency from accessing another agency's documents.
- Allow authorized users to download and preview documents.

10. Immigration forms
- Add the required forms for a case.
- Store the form name, version, status, and assigned employee.
- Fill forms using saved client and applicant information where possible.
- Save form progress without losing previous information.
- Support review, correction, approval, signature, and finalization steps.
- Keep the finalized form and its version attached to the case.

11. Agreements and letters
- Create a service agreement or letter from a template.
- Fill client and case information into the document.
- Allow staff to review and edit the document.
- Save the issued or finalized copy to the case.
- Record when the document was created and issued.

12. Client communication
- Record incoming and outgoing email, SMS, phone, or chat communication.
- Allow staff to add a communication note if a message is sent outside the CRM.
- Link communication to the correct client and case.
- Show communication history in chronological order.
- Clearly separate internal notes from client-visible communication.

13. Client portal
- Allow a client to log in or use a secure invitation.
- Show only the client's own cases, requests, documents, and messages.
- Allow the client to upload requested documents.
- Allow the client to see document or form status.
- Allow the client to send and receive messages.
- Do not expose internal notes, staff-only documents, or other clients.

14. Fees and payments
- Record agreed fees, invoices or charges, payments, refunds, and adjustments.
- Show the total amount, paid amount, and outstanding balance.
- Link billing records to the correct client and case.
- Generate a basic statement of account.
- Restrict financial changes to authorized employees.
- Keep a history of payment changes.

15. Employee dashboard
- Show cases assigned to the logged-in employee.
- Show tasks and follow-ups due today.
- Show overdue tasks, missing documents, and upcoming appointments.
- Show cases waiting for an employee update.
- Each dashboard item should open the correct client or case.

16. Search and navigation
- Search clients by name, phone, email, or client number.
- Search cases by case number, client, case type, status, or employee.
- Open the complete client or case profile from search results.
- Keep the main daily actions easy to find.

17. Basic reports and management view
- Show active, completed, closed, and overdue cases.
- Show cases by type, stage, and assigned employee.
- Show outstanding tasks, follow-ups, and document requests.
- Show fees collected and outstanding balances.
- Add simple filters for date, employee, case type, and status.

18. Basic system controls
- Allow Admin to manage employees and agency information.
- Allow Admin to manage basic workflow and document templates.
- Provide a simple audit or activity log.
- Show clear errors when an action fails.
- Prevent accidental duplicate submissions when a button is clicked twice.

OPTIONAL INTAKE AND LEAD FEATURES FOR THE PROTOTYPE

- Add a potential client or lead manually.
- Convert a lead into a client without entering the same information again.
- Keep the lead source and initial notes after conversion.
- Advanced lead integrations and marketing reports are not required before the Immigration CRM is usable.

MINIMUM END-TO-END TEST BEFORE LIVE USE

1. Log in as an Admin and create an employee.
2. Log in as the employee and confirm another agency's records are not visible.
3. Create a client and confirm duplicate checking works.
4. Create an immigration case for the client.
5. Add the primary applicant and one dependant.
6. Complete and save a basic immigration assessment.
7. Start the correct case workflow and assign tasks.
8. Request a document and upload it through the client portal.
9. Review the document and request a replacement.
10. Add an immigration form, save progress, review it, and finalize it.
11. Create and save a service agreement or client letter.
12. Record a client call, an internal note, and an outgoing message.
13. Schedule a follow-up and an appointment.
14. Confirm due and overdue work appears on the employee dashboard.
15. Record a fee and payment and generate a statement of account.
16. Confirm a Manager or Admin can inspect the complete case history.
17. Complete or close the case and confirm it appears correctly in reports.
18. Confirm the client portal does not show internal notes or staff-only files.

NOT REQUIRED FOR THE INITIAL PROTOTYPE

- AI eligibility decisions or document analysis
- Advanced marketing automation
- Two-way WhatsApp integration
- Call recording and transcription
- Advanced phone-system integration
- Automatic government portal submission
- Full accounting software integration
- Mobile application
- Advanced analytics
- Multiple highly customizable workflows
- Complex notification preferences
- Large-scale performance optimization
- Full production monitoring and high-availability infrastructure

READY TO USE WHEN

A real employee can create a client, manage an immigration case, collect and review documents, complete forms, communicate with the client, track work and payments, and close the case without asking a developer to update the database manually.
